## Scope decisions established against upstream

- `Bisimulation` is intentionally restricted upstream to variables, lambdas, applications and lets (`src/plfa/part2/Bisimulation.lagda.md`, lines 148–175), although the enclosing datatype is imported from `More`. A separate intrinsic lambda/let fragment with a genuine source/translation relation preserves that scope. It need not simulate every constructor of `More`.
- `More` formally develops primitive arithmetic, lets and products on top of the earlier calculus. Sums, unit, empty type and lists are part of its teaching scope and recommended/practice extensions. The audit distinguishes an upstream exercise from an upstream completed theorem.
- De Bruijn names may be introduced earlier than upstream while keeping the external `HasType` judgment of `Lambda`/`Properties` distinct from the intrinsic terms of `DeBruijn`.
- Pointwise equalities are an appropriate constructive replacement for the upstream function-extensionality assumption. They must not be described as intensional equality of arbitrary functions. In particular, `Connectives.currying`, `functionSum` and `functionProduct` currently return `Iff`, supplemented by pointwise round-trip laws.
- Alternative proofs with the same model and result need not reproduce an unused upstream helper lemma. `Compositional.funDown`, `lambdaForward` and `applicationInversion` directly handle `Info`/`Denote` derivations. The owner subsequently also delivered the complete generic `subInv` / `factorAcross` / `arrowInversion` machinery in `Denotational`; it has now been inspected, including transitivity and distribution cases.
